We, the Sun, and Psalms

Curatorial Statement


We, the Sun, and Psalms brings together new work by H.I. Green that traces memory, faith, and the layered inheritance of the Black religious South. The exhibition expands Green’s exploration of origins, longing, and displacement, weaving together painting, sculpture, and mixed media to meditate on the tension between personal devotion and collective history.

Fragments of body and voice, including eyes, mouths, and gestures, become recurring motifs that suggest both surveillance and testimony. In this space, psalms are not only prayers but echoes, where remembrance and resistance form a language of survival and transformation.
